About LifeDrop

Bridging the Gap in Critical Blood Shortages

During accidents, complicated surgeries, and sudden medical emergencies, finding compatible blood quickly is one of healthcare's greatest logistical hurdles.

LifeDrop was engineered to solve this bottleneck by providing a centralized, transparent platform where accredited hospitals and urgent patients can immediately identify, verify, and mobilize ready donors.

For Donors

Update your availability in 1 click and respond when your blood type is required nearby.

For Hospitals

Broadcast critical supply shortages to active donors and track fulfillment in real-time.

Universal Donor & Recipient Facts

  • O-
    Universal Red Cell Donor

    Can be given to patients of any blood type in emergency trauma cases before typing is finished.

  • AB+
    Universal Recipient

    Can safely receive red blood cells from any blood type (A, B, AB, or O).

  • 56d
    Donation Interval

    A healthy adult can safely donate whole blood every 56 days (8 weeks).
